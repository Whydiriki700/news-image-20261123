MONDAY · 23 NOVEMBER 2026
Markets · Wall Street

Huang back at Nvidia, shares soar 31% in best day since 2003

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive 11 days after his resignation, sending the stock up 31% and adding $952 billion in value. The Nasdaq jumped 6.5% and the S&P 500 4.7%, their best day since April 2025.

War · Libya

Ukraine sinks Russian warship off Libya

Sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi port, Zelenskyy said, striking a second Russian ship too and carrying the war far beyond Europe's shores.

Climate · Antalya

COP31 talks collapse with no deal

The UN climate summit ended two days past its deadline with no agreement, the longest climate conference in history, split over fossil fuels and money.

Diplomacy · Shenzhen

Trump calls Putin 'my friend' after summit

Trump met Putin and Xi Jinping in Shenzhen and praised the Russian president, as the White House pressed for a deal to end the war in Ukraine.

Politics · Washington

Democrats win largest House majority since 1992

Democrats won at least 258 House seats, flipping a North Carolina seat to secure their biggest majority in the chamber in 34 years.

Justice · Sinaloa

'El Chapo Isidro' captured, 16 killed in raid

Special forces seized cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores in Sinaloa with US intelligence help. Sixteen suspected cartel members died in the operation.

Conflict · Gulf

Iran vows 'all means' against US blockade

Tehran said it would use all means to counter the US naval blockade and claimed to shoot down a US drone, as explosions were reported in the Strait of Hormuz.

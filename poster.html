War · Libya · Perang

Sea drones sink a Russian corvette far from Europe

Ukrainian sea drones sink the Russian corvette Merkury in the port of Benghazi, President Zelenskyy says. A second Russian ship is also hit, carrying the war far beyond Europe.

Climate · Antalya · Iklim

COP31 collapses with no deal

Climate talks in Antalya fall apart without an agreement, ending two days past deadline as the longest climate conference in history, split over fossil fuels and money.

Diplomacy · Shenzhen · Diplomasi

Trump calls Putin “my friend” after the summit

Trump meets Putin and Xi Jinping in Shenzhen and calls Putin his friend, as the White House presses for a deal to end the war in Ukraine.

Politics · Washington · Politik

Democrats take at least 258 House seats

Democrats win at least 258 seats in the House, flipping one North Carolina seat, for the largest House majority in 34 years, since 1992.

Justice · Sinaloa · Keadilan

Cartel boss El Chapo Isidro captured

Special forces capture cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, known as El Chapo Isidro, with help from US intelligence. Sixteen cartel members are killed in the operation.

Conflict · The Gulf · Konflik

Iran vows to fight a US sea blockade

Iran vows to use every means against a US sea blockade and claims to have shot down a US drone, as explosions are reported in the Strait of Hormuz.
